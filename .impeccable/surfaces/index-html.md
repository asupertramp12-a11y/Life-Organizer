## Scope
The whole app shell (index.html, styles.css, app.js views): Today, Tasks, Habits, Plans, Notes, Settings. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and task
The owner, on a Pixel 10 Pro Fold: cover screen (~410px) for quick check-ins and capture, unfolded (~840px near-square) for the week, reading and writing notes. Priority: Plans and Notes, then Today. Must stay light.
